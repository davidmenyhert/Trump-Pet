Trump-Pet — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 12, 2026

Trump-Pet is a satirical virtual pet game. It is an independent parody and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official product of Donald Trump or any government organization.

Information stored on your device

The game uses local storage to remember gameplay progress and settings, including your virtual pet's era, status, progress, and selected items. This information is stored on your device using the app's local preferences and is not uploaded to a Trump-Pet-operated game server.

Advertising and third-party services

The app integrates Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to display advertisements. Google and its partners may process information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, app interactions, and approximate location derived from network information to deliver, measure, and protect ads, subject to applicable laws and user choices.

For information on Google's data practices, see Google Privacy Policy and AdMob guidance.

Internet access

Trump-Pet requires an internet connection during gameplay. The connection is used for network availability checks and third-party advertising. The app does not require a Trump-Pet account.

Children and advertising

Trump-Pet is not specifically designed for children. Advertising settings and any consent mechanisms must be configured according to the app's actual audience, region, and Google Play/AdMob requirements.

Your choices

You can manage advertising-related privacy options through your device settings and applicable Google consent interfaces. You can remove locally stored game data through your device's app storage settings or by uninstalling the app, subject to your device's backup behavior.

Data security and retention

Local gameplay data remains on your device until it is cleared or the app is removed, subject to device backups. Third-party advertising data is handled under the relevant provider's policies.

Updates

This policy may be updated when the game's features or data practices change. The effective date above will be revised.

Contact

For privacy inquiries, please use the contact channel listed on the Trump-Pet Google Play Store developer page.
